Operating principles / 04

Less theatre.
More signal.

Good work should be understandable, testable and useful. The process stays deliberately simple: find the constraint, run the smallest meaningful test, and scale only when the evidence supports it.

Read the validation principle ↗
01

Find the real constraint

Understand the user, funnel and business problem before choosing a tool or channel.

02

Build the smallest useful system

Connect only the pieces needed to test the core assumption.

03

Measure what changes

Track outcomes, not activity. Keep the limits and uncertainty visible.

04

Improve or move on

Turn repeatable wins into systems — and stop experiments that do not earn their complexity.
