B2B SaaS / SEO / Paid Acquisition / Growth Operations

From individual execution to a multi-channel growth system.

A historical case study covering the growth of social media marketing products for overseas small businesses — connecting SEO, paid ads, communities, attribution, customer conversion and team operations.

01 / Business context

Product portfolio and role

The business served overseas SMBs with multiple social media marketing products across WhatsApp, Twitter/X, LINE and Facebook. The product portfolio covered social publishing, messaging, automation and related marketing workflows, with a focus on Southeast Asia, the United States and Arabic-speaking markets.

Across an approximately three-year period, my role expanded from hands-on channel execution to growth strategy, channel development, measurement, team management and multi-product promotion. The team reached around eight people across SEO, paid acquisition and social operations.

02 / SEO foundation

Connect search demand to paying customers

  • Research markets, competitors and search intent; structure product, feature and use-case keyword clusters.
  • Build content planning, publishing, optimization and technical SEO routines.
  • Monitor organic traffic, rankings, inquiries and paid conversion as separate stages of the funnel.

During a historical stable operating period, SEO averaged around 30,000 monthly UV, with approximately 200–300 Google Top 10 keywords and about 1,000 inquiries per month. The channel generated dozens of paying customers monthly.

03 / Paid acquisition & localization

Structure campaigns around intent and market context

Google Ads campaigns were organized around product terms, feature terms, use cases, competitor brands and alternatives. Campaigns were further separated by country or region so that keywords, local-language copy, bids, budgets and landing pages could reflect different search behavior and acquisition conditions.

Localization work covered Brazil, Saudi Arabia and Malaysia. Click-through rate, conversion rate and inquiry cost improved during the work, but no verified percentage uplift is retained for public reporting.

04 / Growth operations

Build a repeatable operating loop

The work connected acquisition channels with tracking, CRM attribution, reporting, sales follow-up and customer retention. As responsibilities expanded, I coordinated channel priorities, budget allocation, growth targets and team collaboration alongside hands-on SEO and Google Ads execution.

The lesson: a growth system is not a collection of tools or channels. It is the operating link between demand, acquisition, conversion, sales feedback and the next optimization cycle.
