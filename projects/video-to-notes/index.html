Knowledge Workflow / Validation

Turn video links into searchable knowledge.

A workflow concept for researching business cases, overseas growth tactics and reusable ideas. The intended experience is simple: paste a link and receive a structured note — without manually downloading, transcribing and organizing the video.

01 / Link access

Reliable retrieval

Test whether Douyin and TikTok links can be parsed consistently, including login, regional and access restrictions.

02 / Content extraction

Transcript and visual context

Check subtitle availability and accuracy, and identify information that exists only in the video frames.

03 / Note quality

Structured Markdown

Capture summaries, key claims, actionable steps, figures and source links while distinguishing source statements from model interpretation.

04 / User value

Time saved and reuse

Measure whether the output is useful enough to save, search and reuse — not merely a copied transcript.

Minimum useful output

  • Original video URL, title, creator and extraction time when available.
  • A concise summary with key ideas and actionable steps.
  • Important figures, entities and source references.
  • Clear labels for missing or uncertain information; no invented details.
  • A standard Markdown file that works without a proprietary knowledge base.
First validation scope: Douyin and TikTok. Test a balanced sample of videos with and without subtitles, dense narration and important on-screen information before expanding platform coverage.
