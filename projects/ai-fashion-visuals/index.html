AI / E-commerce / Experiment

Product imagery must be useful, not just beautiful.

An experiment in generating model imagery from merchant-provided apparel photos. The goal is to test whether the product itself can be represented faithfully — and whether the workflow can reduce production time and cost.

What we evaluate

Product fidelity

Color accuracy, print consistency, garment details, composition and suitability for real product listings.

Early observation

Fast output, human review

Initial tests produced candidate images quickly, but fine print details could still drift from the source. Product-grade replication is not yet established.

Next validation

Measure usable output

Run blind reviews across SKUs with different colors and print complexity; track acceptance rate, correction time and repeatability before scaling.

Current status: early experiment. Results should not be interpreted as a production guarantee or as evidence of fully automated, exact product reproduction.
