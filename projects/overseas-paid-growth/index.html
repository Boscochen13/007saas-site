Mobile Apps / Paid Acquisition

Make creative, budgets and data work as one optimization loop.

A practical record of overseas mobile app acquisition: testing creatives under payback constraints, refining campaign decisions and connecting channel data with day-to-day operating workflows.

Market expansion

Localize the testing hypothesis

Explore Brazil-market messaging and creative angles through competitor research and localized user context.

Campaign optimization

Respond to creative fatigue

Identify declining creative performance, review recent data and adjust budgets, audiences and campaign strategy without overreacting to a single-day fluctuation.

Data automation

Reduce repetitive reporting

Standardize naming and reporting dimensions, combine channel data and reserve human attention for anomalies and new tests.

Operating principles

  • Separate markets and products where measurement definitions differ.
  • Use creative testing to generate new hypotheses rather than repeatedly scaling a fatigued asset.
  • Evaluate ROI only with a clearly defined revenue window and attribution scope.
  • Automate repetitive data preparation, but keep budget and policy-sensitive decisions reviewable.
The examples are anonymized historical practices, not a promise of campaign performance or a claim that every test was profitable.
